Key observations:
- **"What Did Ancient Humans Do ...?" is the engine.** Ink and Zenn both run it. Ink's version hit 10M and its sibling "Rained All Week" 1.5M. Zenn's "All Day" (437K) and "Actually Eat" (884K) are 16-33x its median.
- **Zenn's second engine is the named "X Effect"** (Calhoun 4.9M, Slotow 321K, Minnesota 103K). Short ones (Nocebo 4 min, Pratfall 4 min, IKEA 4 min) did poorly (13K-52K), so depth matters. Hits were 8-12 min.
- **Mack's volume strategy mostly fails.** ~27 of 30 videos sit at 3-16K at 20-30 min each. Only cold/sleep/survival-flavored titles broke out. Daily uploads dilute, not compound.
- **Zenn's long tail is flat** (13-40K typical). The channel lives on a few hits.
- **Ink is the best performer relative to its size**: median 310K at 109K subs, so the audience is non-subscriber / suggested traffic, driven by title+thumbnail rather than loyalty.

## 3. What drives virality (from the data)
1. **Time-travel "day in the life" question with a situation.** "Do All Day", "Rained All Week", "Survive Winter", "Sleep When Freezing". A mundane situation + ancient humans. The viewer imagines their own day in that world.
2. **Survival and discomfort.** Cold, rain, predators, sleep. Every Mack breakout is a cold/sleep/frozen-north video. Ink's winter, rain and travel videos are all >780K.
3. **"Actually" / "Disturbing" / "TERRIFYING" modifiers** on Ink's titles; they signal a counter-intuitive answer.
4. **Named-phenomenon curiosity** (Calhoun, Slotow, Minnesota). Unknown term the viewer has to click to decode.
5. **Existential identity questions.** "Why Are We the Only Human Species Left?" (1.2M). Zenn's "Are You Real?" / Mack's "Do Humans Really Exist?" did NOT work (30K / 2.7K), so abstract philosophy alone is weak; it needs a concrete ancient-human anchor.
6. **Substance of length:** the 8-12 minute band is the sweet spot for Ink/Zenn hits. Mack's 22-30 min videos mostly flop except on the three winners.
7. **Faceless, low-cost production** lets Ink/Zenn publish 3-6 per month.

## 5. Gaps and opportunities

1. **Situation x hardship combos nobody has done.** The winners are "Ancient humans + a specific bad situation". Open situations: heat wave / drought, a broken bone or infection, childbirth, going blind, losing a tooth, getting lost, a volcanic winter, a flood, being alone, a pregnant winter. Ink only did rain, winter, predators, and Mack did freezing/sleep. Sickness was covered once (Ink, 38K, "when they got Sick") with a flat title; the winning "Disturbing/Actually/When It Rained All Week" style was not used, so it is a title-execution gap, not a topic gap.
2. **Night + sleep + cold is proven on Mack**, but at 28 min on a channel with a weak thumbnail/brand. A tighter 9-11 min version is unclaimed.
3. **"Why Are We the Only ___ Left"-style species/identity videos** (1.2M for Ink) have only one entry. Related: What happened to Neanderthals, Denisovans, Flores "hobbits", what if another species survived, "Could Neanderthals have beaten us?".
4. **Named effects for the ancient-brain angle.** Zenn's effects are modern psychology. Nobody pairs a named effect with ancient survival ("The Savanna Effect", "The Fire-Watch Effect") to merge both engines.
5. **Weak-spot titles to improve on**: Ink's "What did Early Humans do when they got Sick" (lowercase, no modifier, 38K) and Mack's near-duplicate titles (two "Taxes" videos, two "Alcohol", two "Freezing Winters") cannibalize each other.
6. **Packaging gap:** Ink gets 3-10M/video from titles alone but has no repeating series structure or end-screen funnel (109K subs on 10M+ views). A channel with a clear series ("Ancient Humans: Day X") could convert more viewers to subs.

## 6. Plan for the new channel
- **Positioning:** "Survival day-in-the-life" for ancient humans, with one hardship per video. Target 8-11 min, 2 uploads per week at launch (not daily).
- **First 8 titles to test** (each follows the winning pattern: ancient humans + situation + "Actually"/"Disturbing" modifier):
  1. What Did Ancient Humans Do When They Broke a Bone?
  2. What Did Ancient Humans Actually Do During a Heat Wave?
  3. The Disturbing Ways Ancient Humans Gave Birth
  4. What Did Ancient Humans Do When Someone Got Sick and Nobody Could Help?
  5. How Did Ancient Humans Survive a Volcanic Winter?
  6. What Did Ancient Humans Do When They Got Lost Alone?
  7. Could Neanderthals Have Beaten Us? (species/identity engine)
  8. The Fire-Watch Effect: Why You Wake Up at 3 AM (named effect + ancient angle)
- **Avoid:** philosophy titles, invention/economics titles, <6 min videos, 25+ min videos, and exact duplicates of "All Day" / "Actually Eat".
- **Test method:** publish 8, rank by views vs. your own median after 2 weeks, double down on the pattern with the top 2 outlier scores, and retire the rest.

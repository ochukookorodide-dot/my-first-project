## Video 1: "Why 3 AM? What Your Body Is Doing While You Lie Awake"

*Previous title: "Why You Wake Up at 3 AM (Your Ancestors Did Too)", identical copies by four other channels on 2026-10-15. The ancestor angle moves into the hook and the first minute, not the title.*

### The angle and the honest twist
Competitors split into two camps: the "two sleeps is natural" camp (Half as Interesting 2.8M, History by Mae 4.3M, Thoughty2 1.7M) and Zenn's "what did they do at night" (8.0M). Almost nobody tells the full story, which is the differentiator:

- **Historian Roger Ekirch** documented "first sleep / second sleep" in pre-industrial Europe, but that is historical records, not prehistoric evidence.
- **Wehr (1992)**: volunteers kept on 14 hours of darkness a day drifted into two sleeps with a quiet waking in the middle ([source search](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8c58170ccbcdbfafa95cd1ef967652ed48b40b71)).
- **Yetish et al. (2015)**: the Hadza, San and Tsimane did **not** show a clear biphasic pattern; they slept one main block of about 5.7-7.1 hours, with little napping ([Current Biology](https://www.cell.com/fulltext/S0960-9822(15)01157-4)).

So the ancestor promise (said in the hook, not the title) should be delivered as: **broken, flexible, light sleep at night is normal and very old; the neat "two sleeps" story is only part of the truth.** Saying this plainly is the credibility edge over the clones.

### Title / thumbnail text (no thumbnail image analysis was possible)
- Title: Why 3 AM? What Your Body Is Doing While You Lie Awake (nearest existing title similarity 0.60 on 2026-10-15; a similar-framed 26-minute video has 94K views, so the framing has demand)
- Backup titles: "The 3 AM Wake-Up Isn't a Malfunction" (close variants exist, e.g. "Waking at 3AM Isn't a Malfunction", so use only as a fallback). "Why You Wake Up at 3 AM (Your Ancestors Did Too)" is retired.
- Thumbnail text: "3 AM?" or "NOT BROKEN". One clear image: a person awake in a dark room; ancestors by a fire behind them.

### Hook (first 30 seconds), two versions to test
**Version A (relatable, direct):**
> It's 3:07 in the morning. You're awake. Nothing woke you up. Your mind is just... on. And you've probably been told something is wrong with you. But for most of human history, this exact moment was completely normal. Tonight I'll show you what your ancestors did at 3 AM, what the science actually says, and one thing most videos about this get wrong.

**Version B (curiosity gap, myth-bust):**
> You've heard that humans used to sleep in two shifts. It's in a hundred videos. But when scientists measured how hunter-gatherers actually sleep today, they did not find that. So why do you wake up at 3 AM? And what does that tell us about how our ancestors slept? Let's start at midnight.

Recommended: A for the title, B as a mid-video re-hook. Keep the payoff about the "one thing videos get wrong" for minute 6-7.

### Shorts cut (30-45 s)

## Video 4: "Neanderthals Weren't Wiped Out. They Were Outnumbered."

*Previous title: "Neanderthals Didn't Go Extinct. They Were Absorbed." Another channel posted "Neanderthals Didn't Go Extinct. They Became You." and a third used "They're inside you", so both phrasings are retired. Nearest match to the new title is 0.52 similarity, with no real overlap.*

### The angle
The famous videos are about 9 years old (Be Smart "Why Are We The Only Humans Left?" 8.2M, Sam O'Nella 8.6M, both 2017). ExtinctZoo (2024, 7.2M, 16 min) is the modern competitor, and TED-Ed's "biggest myths about Neanderthals" is 522K. A counter-intuitive claim with modern genetics gives a clear reason to click.

**Title fit:** "Outnumbered" is closer to the evidence than "absorbed": the leading model is that small Neanderthal groups were diluted by repeated contact with larger, growing modern human populations, which still leaves room for climate, competition and disease. Say "leading hypothesis" or "one model suggests" in the video, not "settled". Avoid the retired phrasings ("They Became You", "They're inside you").

### Facts to anchor (spot-checked)
| Claim | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Neanderthal tools (Mousterian) end by ~41,030-39,260 years ago; Neanderthals and modern humans overlapped ~2,600-5,400 years | Higham et al. 2014, Nature ([link](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature13621)) | Good |
| "Oase 1" (Romania, 37,000-42,000 years ago) had 6-9% Neanderthal DNA and a Neanderthal ancestor 4-6 generations back | Fu et al. 2015, Nature ([link](https://reich.hms.harvard.edu/sites/reich.hms.harvard.edu/files/inline-files/2015_Nature_Fu_Oase_1.pdf)) | Good |
| Oase 1 did not contribute much to later Europeans (so interbreeding happened more than once and not all lineages persisted) | Fu et al. 2015 | Good |
| Recent modeling: near-complete genetic replacement is possible within 10,000-30,000 years through repeated small-scale immigration ("genetic dilution") | Sci Rep 2025 ([link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-22376-6)) | One model; present as "one model suggests" |
| Non-Africans carry roughly 1-2% Neanderthal DNA | Green et al. 2010 and later work | **Verify exact range before saying a number** |
| Some inherited Neanderthal variants affect immune response and, in one case, COVID-19 severity risk | Zeberg & Pääbo 2020 | **Verify** |
| Svante Pääbo's 2022 Nobel Prize for work on ancient genomes | Nobel Committee | **Verify** |

### Hook, two versions
**Version A (personal, striking):**
> If you have ancestors outside Africa, a small piece of you is Neanderthal. Not metaphorically. In your DNA. So when people say Neanderthals went extinct, what exactly went extinct? Because the evidence is starting to suggest the answer is a lot stranger.

**Version B (provocative, myth-bust):**
> We've been told a simple story: modern humans arrived, Neanderthals lost, and they vanished. But the genetic evidence says something else happened. Not a war. Not a sudden disaster. Something much quieter.

Recommended: A, since the personal hook (it's in you) is the unique edge over the 2017 videos.

### Retention beats
- Open with the personal hook, then delay the full answer to ~4:35.
- Use a recurring question ("what exactly went extinct?") to bind chapters.
- Include one clear, simple graphic for "small group + big group = lineage fades" as the memorable moment (and a possible Short).

### Do not claim
- That Neanderthals were "absorbed" as settled fact; use "leading hypothesis" or "one model suggests".
- Any exact percentage for modern humans without confirming the source.
- That Neanderthals were primitive or dumb (the 2017 videos' tone; modern consensus is more nuanced).
- Violence/genocide narratives as fact.

### Shorts cut (30-45 s)
> If you have ancestors outside Africa, you carry Neanderthal DNA. They didn't just disappear. They met us, had children, and slowly dissolved into a bigger population. Here's why that matters.
